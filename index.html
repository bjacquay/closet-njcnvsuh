    Storage tips

    • Hang heavyweight tees, hoodies, and crews on matching slim hangers, sorted by color.
    • File-fold everyday tees upright in shelf bins instead of stacking them. Stack pressure is what causes the wrinkles.
    • Roll gym shorts and drop them in one bin.
    • Pull clothes from the dryer right away and shake them out.
